\section*{Example}

Water solubility in silicate melts is well constrained
\citep{Silver1989}. Rhyolite-MELTS and its fluid-saturation model
\citep{Gualda2012,Ghiorso2015} are widely used; see also
\citet{Nittler2018} for the chemistry of Mercury and \citet{Roberti2021}
for volcanic landslides. Software, datasets and web pages are cited the same
way \citep{QGIS2024,GVP2013,BRGMweb}.
